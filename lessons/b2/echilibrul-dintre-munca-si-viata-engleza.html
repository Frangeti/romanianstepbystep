2. Reading · Finding a better balance

Many employees struggle to maintain a healthy balance between their professional and personal lives. Although technology allows people to work from almost anywhere, it has also made it more difficult to disconnect. Emails and messages can arrive at any time, creating the impression that employees must always be available.

Some companies have introduced flexible arrangements, such as remote work or shorter Fridays. These measures can improve job satisfaction and productivity. However, flexibility is not always enough. Employees also need to learn how to set boundaries, prioritize tasks and take regular breaks.

Experts believe that a healthy balance benefits both employees and employers. Workers are less likely to suffer from burnout, while companies may experience lower staff turnover and better performance. The most effective solution is probably a shared responsibility: employers must create realistic expectations, and employees must communicate their needs clearly.

4. Grammar · Modal verbs of deduction

Use modal verbs to show how certain you are about a present situation.

CertaintyPatternExample
almost certainmust + verbShe must be exhausted.
possiblemight / may / could + verbHe might be working from home.
impossible or unlikelycan't + verbThey can't be satisfied with this schedule.

1. She has answered emails all night. She ___ be tired.

2. He is not answering his phone. He ___ be in a meeting.

3. They have just started work. They ___ be on holiday already.

5. Speaking · A realistic policy

Role A · Employee

Explain which part of your routine causes stress. Ask for one practical change and justify it with an example.

Role B · Manager

Listen, ask two follow-up questions and propose a compromise. Use In my opinion, On the other hand and A possible solution would be.

Ghidul cerințelor · În română

Citire: citește textul și caută ideea principală și detaliile care o susțin.

Gramatică: folosește must pentru certitudine, might/may/could pentru posibilitate și can't pentru imposibilitate.

Vorbire: lucrează în perechi; explică problema, pune întrebări și propune un compromis în engleză.

Scriere: redactează 180–220 de cuvinte despre avantajele și dezavantajele săptămânii de lucru de patru zile și încheie cu opinia ta.

Test: alege un răspuns pentru fiecare întrebare și apasă butonul de verificare.
